## Script structure for EVERY video (owner's rule, 14 Nov: never skip)
Every video script follows these 6 beats, in this order (diagram: `reels/_brand/script-structure.jpg`):
1. **Pain** – open on the viewer's problem in frame 1, with the tool on screen and a gap they recognise
   from their own account (e.g. Shopify orders piling up, Google Ads at 0 conversions).
2. **Poke** – twist the knife: what the problem is costing them right now (money, leads, reach), so it hurts more.
3. **Fix** – the steps, one idea per scene.
4. **Payoff** – show the result after the fix, working (numbers moving, the green state). The high point.
5. **Recap** – a quick one-scene run-through of all the steps (checklist ticking through), so it's saveable.
6. **CTA** – one clear ask (send / save / comment KEYWORD), then Follow.
Before writing any voiceover, label each line with its beat. If a beat is missing, the script isn't done.
Details and pacing: `reels/PLAN.md`.
