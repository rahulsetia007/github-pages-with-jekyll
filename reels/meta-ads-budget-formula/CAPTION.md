# Meta ads budget formula captions: clicks but no sales? It might be your budget

## Instagram Reel
Running Meta ads but getting clicks and no sales? 🤔 It might not be your ad. It might be your budget.

Meta needs about 50 conversions a week, per ad set, to learn who buys. Below that, your ad set gets stuck in "Learning limited", and Meta says those ad sets pay about 28% more per sale.

The formula:
1️⃣ Find your cost per result (say $20 a sale)
2️⃣ × 50 ÷ 7 = your minimum daily budget per ad set ($20 × 50 ÷ 7 ≈ $143/day)
3️⃣ Can't afford it? Run 1 ad set, not 5. Or optimize for an earlier event like Add to cart ($5 × 50 ÷ 7 ≈ $36/day)

Clicks But No Sales on Meta Ads? It Might Be Your Budget

**Description**
How much should you spend on Meta ads? Meta needs about 50 conversions a week per ad set to exit the learning phase. Meta says ad sets below that pay about 28% more per sale.

THE FORMULA:
Minimum daily budget per ad set = (cost per result × 50) ÷ 7
Example: $20 per sale → $20 × 50 = $1,000 → ÷ 7 ≈ $143 a day.

Can't afford it?
1. Run fewer ad sets: weekly budget ÷ (cost per result × 50) = how many ad sets you can afford.
2. Or optimize for an earlier event that happens more often, like Add to cart ($5 each → about $36 a day). Switch back to Purchase once you get 50+ purchases a week.

Rules of thumb, not laws. Watch your own numbers.
