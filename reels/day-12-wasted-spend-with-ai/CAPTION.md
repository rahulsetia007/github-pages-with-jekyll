## YouTube Shorts
**Title** (74 chars)
Find Wasted Google Ads Spend in 5 Minutes with ChatGPT (Negative Keywords)

**Description**
Stop checking search terms by hand. Export your report, run one ChatGPT prompt, and get a clean list of negative keywords grouped by theme. Always check it before adding.

How to do it:
1️⃣ In Google Ads, download your search terms report as a CSV (remove client names)
2️⃣ Upload it to ChatGPT with the prompt: it finds the junk and groups it by theme (jobs, free, DIY…)
3️⃣ Check the list yourself, then add the negatives. One wrong negative can block real customers.

THE PROMPT:
Here's the prompt 👇

STEP 1: In Google Ads, go to Insights and reports → Search terms. Set the last 30 days, then download as CSV. Remove client names.

STEP 2: Upload the file to ChatGPT with this prompt:

"This is my Google Ads search terms report for [business]. List the search terms that are clearly not potential customers (jobs, courses, free, DIY, wrong location, competitors). Group them into themes and suggest negative keywords for each theme. Don't include anything that could be a real customer."

STEP 3: Check the list yourself before adding anything. AI can be wrong, and one bad negative can block real customers.
