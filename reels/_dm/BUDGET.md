Here's the Meta ads budget formula 👇

Minimum daily budget per ad set =
(cost per result × 50) ÷ 7

Why 50? Meta needs about 50 conversions a week per ad set to exit the learning phase. Meta says ad sets under that pay about 28% more per sale.

Example: $20 per sale → $20 × 50 = $1,000 → ÷ 7 = about $143 a day.

Can't afford it?
1. Run fewer ad sets. Your weekly budget ÷ (cost per result × 50) = how many ad sets you can afford.
2. Or optimize for an earlier event that happens more often, like Add to cart ($5 each → about $36 a day). Switch back to Purchase once you're getting 50+ purchases a week.

Don't have a cost per result yet? Use your best guess (e.g. your product price × 30%) and update it after a week.

Rules of thumb, not laws. Watch your own numbers.
