# Ad to page promise match captions: clicks but nobody buys?

## Instagram Reel
Getting clicks, but nobody buys? 🍕👟 Your ad might be making a promise your page doesn't keep.

Picture a shop sign that says pizza. You walk in, and they sell shoes. You walk straight out, and that's what your visitors do after you've paid for the click.

The fix:
1️⃣ Write down the one promise in your ad (the offer, the price, the words). Open your page: can you see that same promise at the top, right away?
2️⃣ If not, copy your ad's headline to the top of your page. Same words, same offer, same picture.
3️⃣ One ad, one page. If your ad is about one product, don't send people to your home page.
