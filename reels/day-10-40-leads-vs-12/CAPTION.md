# Day 10 captions: Google Ads says 40 leads, your inbox says 12

## Instagram Reel
Google Ads says 40 leads. Your inbox says 12 🤔

Usually it's one lead counted two or three times. Google then bids for more "fake" leads, and you scale a campaign that isn't working.

How to fix it (2 minutes, in Google Ads → Goals → Conversions):
1️⃣ Keep ONE real lead action as primary (e.g. your form submit)
2️⃣ Set duplicates like the thank-you page view to secondary
3️⃣ For lead actions, change Count from "Every" to "One"

Now your dashboard matches your inbox, and Smart Bidding learns from real leads.
