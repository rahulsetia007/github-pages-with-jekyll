# Day 9 captions: 7 Google Ads settings burning your budget

## Instagram Reel
Google Ads budget gone by lunch and zero leads? 🔥

Check these 7 settings. Each one takes about a minute:

1️⃣ Location: switch "Presence or interest" to "Presence" (campaign settings → Locations)
2️⃣ Networks: turn off the Display Network in Search campaigns
3️⃣ Negatives: add negative keywords every week (jobs, free, DIY, salary)
4️⃣ Goals: don't count page views as leads; track real calls and forms
5️⃣ Auto-apply: turn off auto-applied recommendations and review them yourself
6️⃣ Structure: one ad group per service, not one for everything
7️⃣ Schedule: only run ads when someone can answer the phone
