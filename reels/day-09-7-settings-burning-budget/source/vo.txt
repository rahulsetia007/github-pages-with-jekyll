r1|One. Location. Switch Presence or interest to Presence, so only locals see your ads.
r2|Two. Networks. In a Search campaign, turn off the Display Network.
r3|Three. Negatives. Add negative keywords every week, like jobs, free, and DIY.
r4|Four. Goals. Don't count page views as leads. Track real calls and forms.
r5|Five. Auto-apply. Turn off auto-applied recommendations, and review them yourself.
r6|Six. Structure. One ad group per service, not one for everything.
r7|Seven. Schedule. Only run ads when someone can answer the phone.
