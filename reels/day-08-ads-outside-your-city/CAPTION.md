# Day 8 captions: Google Ads showing outside your city

## Instagram Reel
Your Google Ads might be showing in other cities 📍

The default location option can include people who are just interested in your area, even if they live hours away. They click, you pay, and they can never buy.

How to check it (2 minutes):
1. Open your campaign → Settings
2. Locations → Location options
3. Under "Include", pick "Presence: People in or regularly in your included locations"
4. Repeat for every campaign (each has its own setting)

Exception: if you sell to visitors (hotels, tours, wedding venues), the default "Presence or interest" can make sense.
