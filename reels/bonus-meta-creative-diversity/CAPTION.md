## Instagram Reel
Meta is now grading how different your ads look 👀

Meta Ads Manager now rates each ad set on creative diversity: how visually different your images and videos are from each other (Low, Medium or High).

✅ How to see it: Ads Manager → Ad sets → Columns → Customize columns → search "Creative diversity" (still rolling out)
✅ It's NOT about more creators. 5 people in the same kind of ad still looks the same.
✅ It IS about mixing styles: product shot, comparison, UGC, lifestyle, before/after, demo
⚠️ Meta marks it as estimated and in development. Test for 2–4 weeks and judge by cost per result, not the score.
