# Form friction captions: every extra box is a reason to leave

## Instagram Reel
People start filling in your form, and then they quit. 🔒 Every extra box is a reason to leave.

Think of your form like a front door: each field is another lock. Add enough locks and people give up, after you've paid to bring them to the door.

How to cut the locks:
1️⃣ List every field. For each one ask: do I need this before my first reply?
2️⃣ Cut everything that's a no (company size, budget, "how did you hear about us"). Ask those later.
3️⃣ Keep only what you need to reply: usually a name, an email or phone, and one question. Then send yourself a test lead and time it.
