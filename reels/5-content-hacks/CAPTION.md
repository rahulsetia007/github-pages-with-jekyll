| Hack | Est. saved / week | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Batch filming | ~3h | One setup, lighting and outfit change instead of four |
| Repurpose | ~2h | One long video becomes a reel, carousel and newsletter |
| Templates | ~1.5h | No designing covers and captions from scratch |
| Schedule the week at once | ~1h | No daily posting, no context-switching |
| Idea bank | ~0.5h | No staring at a blank page |
| **Total** | **~8h** | |

These are typical estimates, not results from a study. Adjust if your workflow differs.
