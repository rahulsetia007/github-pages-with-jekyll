# Day 7 captions: 1 video = 5 posts

## Instagram Reel
Posting every day and still burnt out? Stop starting from scratch 👇

Most creators make every post from a brand-new idea, then post each video once. A couple of days later it's buried. Reuse it instead: 1 video, 5 posts.

Copy this prompt into ChatGPT:
"Turn this video script into 5 posts: an Instagram caption, a 5-slide carousel (one line per slide), a LinkedIn post, a 4-post X thread and a short email. Keep each one simple and in my voice. Script: [paste your script]"

What it gave me from my last Reel:
• Carousel: "Stuck at 200 views?" → "Every Reel goes to a small test group" → "Swipe in 3 seconds = no push" → "Lead with their problem" → "Copy my hook prompt"
• LinkedIn: "My Reels were stuck at 200 views for months. The fix wasn't posting more. It was my first 3 seconds."
• Email subject: "Why your videos stall at 200 views"

📩 Send this to a creator who's always out of ideas
