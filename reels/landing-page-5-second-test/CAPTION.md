# Landing page 5-second test captions

## Instagram Reel
People click your ad, land on your page, and leave in seconds. 🪟 Your page might be failing the 5-second test.

Your landing page is a shop window: people glance for a few seconds, then walk in or walk past.

The 5-second test:
1️⃣ Clear: show your page to a friend for 5 seconds. Can they say what you sell and who it's for?
2️⃣ Proof: a review, a number or a real customer near the top. If not, add one
3️⃣ No friction: one obvious button. Remove menus, pop-ups and extra links that pull people away
