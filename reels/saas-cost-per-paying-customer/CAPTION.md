**Title** (71 chars)
SaaS Ads: The 5-Minute Monday Routine (Cost Per Paying Customer, Not Trials)

**Description**
Google and Meta count trials as conversions. Billing counts customers. This routine gives every campaign one clear call each Monday: scale it, fix it, hold it, or get more data.

THE ROUTINE:
STEP 1: Download last week's spend, signups and paid customers (from billing), by campaign.

STEP 2: Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT with this prompt:

"For each campaign, calculate cost per paying customer and trial-to-paid rate. Then give ONE call per campaign using these rules: SCALE if cost per paying customer is under [your target]. FIX if trials went up but paying customers stayed flat. HOLD if fewer than 10 paying customers. GET DATA if ad platform signups and billing differ by more than 20%. Show the numbers behind each call."

STEP 3: Act on the calls. Fix tracking before you trust any other call.

Rules of thumb, not laws: set your own target from what a customer is worth to you.
