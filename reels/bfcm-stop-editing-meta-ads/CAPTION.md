**Description**
Meta says ad sets under ~50 conversions a week pay about 28% more per purchase, and big edits can push ad sets back into learning right before Black Friday. Here's the fix:

THE CHECKLIST:
1. Do the math: weekly budget ÷ (cost per purchase × 50) = how many ad sets you can afford. Example: $3,500 ÷ ($35 × 50) = 2 ad sets.
2. Merge down to that number TODAY so they finish learning before the sale.
3. Schedule your Black Friday budget: Ads Manager → budget → Budget scheduling → raise it for Fri–Mon. It goes back by itself after.
4. Hands off until Tuesday: no new ads, no targeting or bid changes, no big budget edits.
5. Check tracking once: run a test purchase and confirm it shows in Events Manager (Pixel + Conversions API).

Rules of thumb, not laws. Watch your own numbers.
