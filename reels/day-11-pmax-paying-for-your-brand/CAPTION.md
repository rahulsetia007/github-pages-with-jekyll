**Title** (63 chars)
Is Performance Max Paying for Your Own Brand Name? How to Check

**Description**
Performance Max can bid on searches for your own business name, so you pay for clicks you'd likely get for free. Here's how to check, and the full checklist below.

How to check (in Google Ads):
1️⃣ Open your Performance Max campaign's search terms and look for your brand name
2️⃣ Open channel performance and see how much went to Search
3️⃣ If it fits your goals, add a brand exclusion for your own name (campaign settings)

FULL CHECKLIST:
Here's your Performance Max checklist 👇

1. Open the channel performance report. See where the money goes: Search, YouTube, Display, Gmail, Maps.
2. Check the search terms. Brand searches eating the budget? Add a brand exclusion list (if it fits your goals).
3. Add negative keywords for junk searches (jobs, free, DIY).
4. Goals: keep only real leads or sales as primary. No page views.
5. Locations: set to "Presence" for local businesses.
6. Final URL expansion: turn it off or exclude pages that shouldn't get traffic (careers, blog).
7. Give it real audience signals: your customer list and website visitors.
8. Wait 2 weeks after big changes before judging.
