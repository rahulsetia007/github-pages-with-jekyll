# Captions: the 6-beat script

## Instagram Reel
Your Reel is dying in the first 2 seconds. Here's how to script it so people stay 👇

Every swipe tells Instagram nobody wants it, so it stops showing your Reel. Use this 6-step script:

1️⃣ Pain: open on their problem, not your intro ("Stuck at 150 views?" beats "Hey guys")
2️⃣ Poke: show what it's costing them
3️⃣ Fix: give the answer, one step per scene
4️⃣ Payoff: show it working (the reward for staying)
5️⃣ Recap: one line they can save
6️⃣ Ask: one clear call to action (save, share or comment)

This Reel follows all 6 steps. Watch it again and spot each beat 👀
