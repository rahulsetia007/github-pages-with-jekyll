# Captions: the 6-beat script

## Instagram Reel
Most of your viewers leave in the first 2 seconds 👇

When they swipe, Instagram stops pushing your Reel. That's why so many Reels get stuck around 150 views. The fix isn't better editing. It's a 6-beat script:

1️⃣ Pain: open on their problem, not your intro ("Stuck at 150 views?" beats "Hey guys")
2️⃣ Poke: show what it's costing them
3️⃣ Fix: give the answer, one step per scene
4️⃣ Payoff: show it working (the reward for staying)
5️⃣ Recap: one line they can save
6️⃣ CTA: one clear ask (save, send or comment)

This Reel follows all 6. Watch it again and spot each beat 👀
