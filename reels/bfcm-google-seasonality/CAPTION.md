# BFCM #2 captions: don't press this Google Ads button before Black Friday

## Instagram Reel
Don't press this Google Ads button before Black Friday. 🛑

It's called a seasonality adjustment. It tells Google to bid higher because you expect more sales. But in an Optmyzr study of 5,000+ accounts, the ones that used it saw click costs rise about twice as much as the ones that didn't.

Do this instead, before Friday:
1️⃣ Type "seasonality adjustments" in the Google Ads search bar. If one is set for Black Friday, remove it
2️⃣ Let Smart Bidding do its job. Google says it already accounts for seasonal events like holidays
3️⃣ Put that energy into your offer: add a Black Friday promotion asset so your discount shows right in the ad

📩 Send this to someone running Google Ads this Black Friday

## YouTube Shorts
**Title** (54 chars)
Don't Press This Google Ads Button Before Black Friday

**Description**
Seasonality adjustments tell Google to bid higher because you expect more sales. But in Optmyzr's study of 5,000+ Google Ads accounts per year (Black Friday–Cyber Monday 2022–2024), accounts that used them saw click costs rise about twice as much as accounts that didn't. Google's own guidance says Smart Bidding already accounts for seasonality.

Do this instead:
1️⃣ Type "seasonality adjustments" in the Google Ads search bar. If one is set for Black Friday, remove it.
2️⃣ Let Smart Bidding do its job.
3️⃣ Add a Black Friday promotion asset so your discount shows right in the ad.

When adjustments DO make sense: a short sale Google can't predict, like a surprise 24-hour flash sale. Black Friday isn't a surprise.

Sources: Optmyzr BFCM seasonality study; Google Ads Help, "About seasonality adjustments".
