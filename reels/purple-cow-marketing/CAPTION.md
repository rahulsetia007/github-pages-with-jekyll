# Purple Cow captions: why nobody stops on your ad

## Instagram Reel
Your ad looks like every other ad in the feed. That's why nobody stops. 🐄

Picture driving past a field of brown cows. After a few minutes, you stop looking. But a purple cow? You'd stop. You'd tell a friend. That's Seth Godin's Purple Cow, and here's how to find yours:

1️⃣ Write down what every competitor says ("quality", "fast shipping", "best price"). Those are your brown cows. Cross them out.
2️⃣ Ask: what would a customer tell a friend about us? Pick one thing and push it to the extreme. Not "fast replies". "Replies in 5 minutes, guaranteed."
3️⃣ Put it in the first line of your ad, and show it to the people who'll care most. They'll spread it for you.
