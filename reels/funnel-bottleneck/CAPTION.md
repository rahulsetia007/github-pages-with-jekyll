# Funnel bottleneck captions: you're fixing the wrong part of your funnel

## Instagram Reel
You keep fixing your ads, but sales don't move? 🚰 You might be fixing the wrong part of your funnel.

Picture a pipe with one big leak. Turning up the tap doesn't help: more ad budget just leaks out faster.

How to find the leak:
1️⃣ Write down each step with last month's numbers: clicks, page visits, add to carts, sales
2️⃣ Divide each step by the one before it. 1,000 clicks → 800 visits (80%) → 200 carts (25%) → 20 sales (10%). The lowest % is your leak
3️⃣ Fix only that step first. Here it's checkout: shipping costs, payment options, too many steps
